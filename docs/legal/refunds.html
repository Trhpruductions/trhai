Cases that are put right whatever the window

  • A duplicate payment. If you were charged twice for one license, the extra charge is refunded.
  • A failed purchase. If you were charged and no license key reached you, you will be sent the key or refunded.
  • A billing error. If the amount charged was not the amount shown on the checkout page, the difference is refunded.
  • An unauthorized transaction. If a charge was made that you did not make, contact your bank or card company straight away and tell us too, so the license it bought can be withdrawn and the payment provider can help.

Your rights under the law

Nothing in this policy takes away a right the law gives you as a consumer. In some countries a buyer has a right to cancel a purchase of digital content within a set time - 14 days in the European Union and the United Kingdom, for example - and in some cases that right ends once you ask for the content to be delivered at once. Other places give rights in other ways, or none for a digital purchase.

For legal review. Which consumer-protection rules apply, and how a license key delivered immediately interacts with them (including any waiver of a cancellation right and how consent to it is obtained), depends on where TRH AI is sold and must be settled with a lawyer.

After a refund

TRH AI checks a license key on your own PC, without the internet, so a key cannot be switched off from outside. When a license is refunded, you agree to stop using it and to remove the key (Settings, Plan and billing). Everything you made with TRH AI stays on your PC and stays yours.

For legal review. A refunded key keeps working until it is removed, because the app has no server to revoke it. A lawyer should confirm that a promise to stop using a refunded license is the right way to handle that.
