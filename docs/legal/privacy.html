1. The short version

In short. TRH AI runs on your own PC. The AI that answers you runs there too. What you write, the files it reads and the answers it gives are stored on your PC, encrypted, and are not sent to TRH Development or to any AI company.
  • TRH Development has no server that your conversations, files or account go to, and no tracking, advertising or analytics in the app.
  • A few features contact other companies' services when you use them: a check for new versions, downloads of the engine, models and voices, web searches, web pages you ask it to read, and email you ask it to send. Section 4 lists every one, what it sends and how to stop it.
  • You can see what is stored (Settings, Data and privacy), copy it into a file, and erase it (Settings, Privacy and consent).
  • No system is perfectly secure. Section 8 says what the protection is, and what it is not.

6. Why information is used

  • To do what you ask: answering, remembering, reading your files and making things for you.
  • To keep you signed in, and to keep one person's conversations apart from another's on the same PC.
  • To check your license and the trial, on your PC, without the internet.
  • To tell you about new versions, and to download what you choose to install.
  • To look after the software: the logs help find why something failed.
  • To keep records the law may require, and to answer your requests.

Information is never used to train or improve any AI model: it does not leave your PC to do so, and the model running on your PC does not learn from it.

7. How the AI uses your information

The model reads what it needs to answer you - your message, earlier parts of the conversation, the memories and documents that match what you asked, and files you point it to - and writes an answer. That all happens on your PC. See How TRH AI uses AI.

A model is a fixed file: it does not remember you between questions. What looks like memory is the conversation and memory kept on your PC, which are given to the model again each time.

8. How information is protected

  • Encrypted storage. The files TRH AI owns are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key on your PC. See section 3 for what that does and does not protect against.
  • Passwords. Only a salted scrypt hash of your password is kept, and hashes of recovery codes and sign-in sessions. They cannot be turned back into the originals.
  • Only your PC can reach it. The service and the app listen on your PC's own address. Other devices cannot connect unless you turn on network access, and then each needs a key.
  • Limits on guessing. Signing in, recovering an account and changing a password are rate-limited.
  • Safety switches. Machine access and network access are switches you control. Machine access is on by default in this version; see How TRH AI uses AI.
  • The window. The app's window is sandboxed: a web page shown in it cannot run programs on your PC, and only the app's own pages may use the microphone.

None of this makes any system perfectly secure. Anyone who can sign in to your Windows account, or who can run programs as you, can read what TRH AI can read. Use a Windows password, and lock your PC.

9. How long information is kept

The table in section 3 gives each period. In short: your information stays on your PC until you remove it. When you erase it, it is deleted from the files and from the one copy of the accounts file that TRH AI makes when it upgrades that file; it is not hidden or marked. Backups you made yourself, such as a copy of the folder, are yours to delete.

For legal review. The retention periods above (30 days for logs, 24 months for records of erasure) are working values chosen by the developer, not decided by the business with a lawyer. Confirm or change them.

10. Your choices and rights

  • See what is stored: Settings, Data and privacy.
  • Get a copy: Settings, Privacy and consent, Copy my data. It is one file you can open, in plain words.
  • Erase it: Settings, Privacy and consent, Erase. You choose what to erase and type a word to confirm; with an account, you also enter your password. A short note says what was and was not removed.
  • Correct it: edit conversations, memories, documents and to-dos in the app. To change an account's name or email, erase the account and make a new one.
  • Say no to optional storage: the choices in Settings, Privacy and consent control the optional settings kept in your browser.
  • Ask us: about information held by TRH Development, write to the privacy address in section 2.

Where the law gives you more - for example to object to processing, to ask for your information to be restricted, or to complain to a data protection authority - those rights are yours too.

For legal review. The specific rights, response deadlines (for example one month under the GDPR, 45 days in some US states) and the authority to complain to depend on where people using TRH AI live. A lawyer must list them.
