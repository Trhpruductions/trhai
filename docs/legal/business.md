## Where the software and its updates come from

Official installers of TRH AI are published as releases at **https://github.com/Trhpruductions/trhai/releases**, together with a list of their SHA-256 checksums (`SHA256SUMS.txt`). The app looks for newer versions at that address and nowhere else. Treat an installer from any other place as unofficial.

The installer is not yet signed with a code-signing certificate, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time you run it. Compare the checksum of what you downloaded with the published list if you want to be sure it is the file that was released.

## Price and keys

TRH AI is free: TRH Development charges nothing for it, and it has no payment screen and never sees a card or bank number. Owner and administrator keys, which let an account look after the other accounts on a copy, are issued by TRH Development; nobody needs one to use the software.
